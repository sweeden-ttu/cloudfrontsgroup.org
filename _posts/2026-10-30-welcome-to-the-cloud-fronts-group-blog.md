Cloud Fronts Group has always worked face to face: a phone call, a coffee, a quick fix to a page that needed it yesterday. This blog is the same conversation, written down so more people can use it.

<!--more-->

## What we'll write about

We keep our focus areas short, and the blog follows them:

- **Growing your business online.** Domain names, web design and hosting, logo and brand design, and the marketing that gets a small business found. Expect checklists you can act on the same afternoon.
- **AI/ML research.** Notes from our work on bounded-agent security guardrails, including the paper we're presenting at AAAI 2026, written for people who run businesses rather than labs.
- **HUD data analytics and market intelligence.** What public housing and foreclosure data actually says about Bell County and Central Texas, with the charts and the caveats.
- **Section 3 compliance.** Plain-language guidance for contractors and agencies that need to meet HUD Section 3 requirements.
- **Community technology access.** Updates from the programs that put devices, connectivity, and training in front of the people who need them.

## Built to be shared

Every post here is written to travel. Each one has a social-ready summary, an image sized for link previews, and share buttons for Facebook, X, LinkedIn, and email at the bottom of the page. If you'd rather automate it, the [RSS feed](../feed.xml) carries the full text of every post, so tools like Buffer or Zapier can pick them up as they publish.
