Domain Name Registration & Management

Your domain name is your digital address — make it count. Register, transfer, and manage your domains with Cloud Fronts Group.

A strong domain name is the foundation of your online presence. It's how customers find you, remember you, and refer you to others. Whether you're launching a new business, rebranding an existing one, or securing domains for future growth, Cloud Fronts Group makes the process simple and affordable.

We offer domain name registration across all major top-level domains (TLDs), including .com, .org, .net, .io, .co, and many more. Need something specialized? We can help with country-code TLDs, industry-specific extensions, and premium domain acquisitions. Our team will guide you to the right choice for your brand.

Our Domain Services

New Registrations

Search availability and register new domains instantly. We'll check hundreds of TLD options to find the perfect fit for your business name and brand.

Domain Transfers

Moving your domains from another provider? We handle the entire transfer process seamlessly, with no downtime and no hassle. We'll even help you consolidate multiple domains under one account.

DNS Management

Full DNS control including A records, CNAME, MX, TXT, and NS records. Point your domain to your website, configure email, set up subdomains, and more — all through an intuitive control panel.

WHOIS Privacy Protection

Keep your personal contact information private. Our WHOIS privacy protection shields your name, address, phone number, and email from public databases, reducing spam and protecting your identity.

Domain Forwarding & Parking

Redirect domains to your main website, set up temporary parking pages, or create domain aliases. Perfect for protecting multiple brand variations and directing traffic where you want it.

Bulk Domain Management

Managing a portfolio of domains? We offer bulk registration, auto-renewal, centralized billing, and account management tools to keep your domain portfolio organized and secure.

Why Your Domain Name Matters

Your domain name is often the first thing potential customers see. A clean, memorable domain builds trust and credibility. A mismatched or hard-to-spell domain can cost you business. We help you choose a domain that's short, easy to type, and aligned with your brand. We also recommend registering common variations and misspellings to protect your brand from typo-squatters and competitors.

Every domain registration includes free DNS management, easy renewal reminders, and access to our support team. Contact us to check domain availability or transfer your existing domains to Cloud Fronts Group.
