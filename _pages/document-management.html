Document Management

Transform your paper-heavy workflow into a streamlined digital operation. Cloud Fronts Group provides comprehensive document management and scanning services for Central Texas businesses transitioning to paperless.

Document Scanning & Digitization

We handle document scanning projects of any scale, from a few boxes of records to warehouse-sized archives. Our high-speed scanning equipment processes thousands of pages per day with exceptional accuracy and image quality. We handle everything from invoices and contracts to medical records and historical documents, converting your physical files into searchable, organized digital assets.

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) & Indexing

A scanned document is just a picture without the right technology. Our OCR processing converts scanned images into fully searchable text, making every word in your documents discoverable in seconds. We pair OCR with professional document indexing, organizing your files by date, client name, document type, or any metadata structure that matches your workflow. Your team will spend less time hunting for files and more time working with them.

Cloud Document Management Systems

Digital documents need a secure, accessible home. We help you select, configure, and deploy cloud-based document management systems that fit your organization's needs. Our solutions include secure file storage with role-based access controls, version tracking, automated backup, and remote accessibility. Whether you need a simple organized folder structure or a full-featured document management platform with workflow automation, we build a system that grows with your business.

Workflow Automation & Secure Storage

Document management is about more than storage—it's about improving how work gets done. We design automated document workflows that route approvals, trigger notifications, and archive completed records without manual intervention. Combined with bank-grade encryption and compliance-ready storage practices, our document management solutions give Central Texas businesses the efficiency of paperless operations with the security and reliability they demand.
