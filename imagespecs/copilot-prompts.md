# Copilot Image Prompts — Missing Requests (9–12)

Use these prompts in Microsoft Copilot (Copilot Designer / Image Creator) to generate the missing opener and mid-content images.

---

## Request 9 — document-management.html (opener) — "Document Workflow"

**Prompt:**
> Left-to-right transformation illustration showing document workflow digitization. Left side: a wooden filing cabinet overflowing with paper piles and a wall clock icon (representing time wasted). Center: a modern document scanner with a glowing blue digital arrow passing through it. Right side: a clean digital folder interface showing Contracts, Invoices, and HR folders with a search bar finding a highlighted file, plus a security shield and lock icon. Below: a speedometer gauge with the needle pointing from red "slow" zone to green "fast" zone. Clean flat vector style, navy blue and gold accent palette, white background.

## Request 10 — document-management.html (mid) — "Cloud Storage & Access"

**Prompt:**

## Request 12 — online-publishing.html (mid) — "Content Repurposing"

**Prompt:**

Spokes radiate out to: blog post icon connected to social media card icons for Instagram, X, and LinkedIn; video icon connected to transcript and podcast icons; webinar icon connected to PDF guide icon; research data icon connected to infographic and slide deck icons.
