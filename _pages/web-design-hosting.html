Web Design & Hosting

Cloud Fronts Group builds custom websites that look great, load fast, and work perfectly on every device. From simple brochure sites to complex e-commerce stores, our web design and development team creates responsive, mobile-first experiences that help Central Texas businesses succeed online.

Our design process starts with your goals. We learn about your audience, your competition, and the actions you want visitors to take. Then we craft a clean, intuitive interface that guides users naturally toward those goals. Every site we build features SEO-friendly semantic HTML, optimized images, minimal HTTP requests, and caching strategies that keep load times under two seconds. We build on platforms that make sense for your business — WordPress for content-rich sites, WooCommerce or Shopify for e-commerce, and custom web applications when you need something unique.

Accessibility is not an afterthought. We design to WCAG 2.1 AA standards, ensuring your site is usable by people of all abilities. We test across browsers and devices, optimize Core Web Vitals, and set up analytics so you can track performance from day one. Launching with Cloud Fronts Group means you get a site that is built right from the ground up.

Every plan includes daily automated backups, free SSL certificates, a global CDN for fast delivery, malware scanning, and 99.9% uptime monitoring. We handle software updates, security patches, and performance tuning so you can focus on running your business. Hosting plans start at $19 per month and scale with your traffic.
