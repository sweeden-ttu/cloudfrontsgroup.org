Custom Promotional Merchandise

Put your brand in your customers' hands. Custom-printed and embroidered promotional products for Central Texas businesses, events, and organizations.

Promotional merchandise is one of the most cost-effective marketing tools available. A branded pen, t-shirt, or coffee mug keeps your business top of mind every time it's used — and unlike a digital ad that disappears with a click, a physical product can last for years. Cloud Fronts Group helps Central Texas businesses create custom promotional items that make a lasting impression.

We source high-quality products from trusted manufacturers and offer a wide range of customization options, including screen printing, embroidery, digital printing, and laser engraving. Whether you need 50 pens for a grand opening or 500 branded t-shirts for a community event, we've got you covered with competitive bulk pricing.

Popular Promotional Products

Apparel

T-shirts, polo shirts, hats, jackets, and vests. Screen printed or embroidered with your logo. Choose from a wide selection of colors, sizes, and styles.

Writing Instruments

Pens, pencils, highlighters, and markers. Imprinted with your brand name and contact info. Perfect for giveaways, conferences, and everyday use.

Drinkware

Mugs, water bottles, tumblers, and travel cups. Custom printed or engraved with your logo. Great corporate gifts and client appreciation items.

Totes & Bags

Shopping totes, drawstring bags, backpacks, and coolers. Custom printed with your brand. Practical and visible — your customers will carry your brand everywhere.

Tech Accessories

USB drives, phone grips, charging cables, and screen cleaners. Custom branded with your logo or message. Popular at trade shows and tech events.

Office & Desk Items

Stress balls, sticky note holders, mouse pads, calendars, and desk organizers. Keep your brand visible in your customers' workspaces every day.

Why Promotional Products Work

Studies show that promotional products generate higher recall rates than almost any other form of advertising. A branded item creates a tangible connection between your customer and your business. Every time someone uses your pen, wears your shirt, or drinks from your mug, they're reminded of your brand — and so is everyone around them. It's passive advertising that keeps working long after the initial giveaway.
