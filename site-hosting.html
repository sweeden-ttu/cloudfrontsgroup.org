Web Hosting Services

Fast, secure, and reliable hosting for Central Texas businesses. From shared plans to dedicated servers, we keep your website online and performing at its best.

Your website needs a reliable home. At Cloud Fronts Group, we provide professional web hosting solutions designed for businesses of all sizes. Whether you're launching a simple brochure site or running a high-traffic e-commerce store, our hosting plans deliver the performance, security, and support you need to succeed online.

All of our hosting plans are backed by a 99.9% uptime guarantee, 24/7 technical support, and state-of-the-art infrastructure. We monitor our servers around the clock to detect and resolve issues before they affect your site. And because we're a local Central Texas company, you get personalized support from people who know you and your business — not a faceless call center.

Our Hosting Plans

Shared Hosting

Perfect for small business websites, blogs, and personal sites. Includes cPanel access, free SSL certificate, daily backups, one-click WordPress installation, and unlimited email accounts. Affordable and easy to manage.

VPS Hosting

Virtual private servers with dedicated resources and full root access. Ideal for growing businesses that need more power and control than shared hosting provides. Scalable CPU, RAM, and storage options available.

Dedicated Servers

Enterprise-grade dedicated servers for high-traffic websites, applications, and resource-intensive workloads. Full administrative access, customized configurations, and priority support included.

SSL Certificates

Free SSL certificates with every hosting plan, plus premium options for e-commerce and business-critical sites. Encrypt customer data, build trust, and improve search engine rankings.

Daily Backups

Automated daily backups with one-click restore. Your data is securely stored off-site and can be recovered quickly in the event of an accident, hack, or server failure.

24/7 Support

Our support team is available around the clock by phone, email, and chat. Whether you need help with a technical issue or just have a question about your account, we're here to help.

Why Host With Cloud Fronts Group?

Every hosting plan includes the features you need to succeed: cPanel for easy management, one-click installs for WordPress, Joomla, and other popular platforms, free website migration, and unmetered bandwidth on most plans. We use enterprise SSD storage for fast load times, and our network is optimized for speed and redundancy.

Moving from another host? We'll migrate your site for free with zero downtime. No long-term contracts, no hidden fees, no surprises. Just reliable hosting from a team that cares about your success. Contact us to find the right hosting plan for your business.
